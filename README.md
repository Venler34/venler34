## 📚 About Me

I'm really interested in solving complex problems where high throughput and speed are critical.   

Previously at Capital One I worked on a semantic search engine that would process millions of customer service transcripts, I redesigned the schema in AWS OpenSearch to speedup throughput leading to 27x faster ingestion speed. At the Trade Desk I worked on managing traffic load for different services by implementing a Horizontal Pod Autoscaler. This helped reduce resource usage by 33% by only having clusters available when needed.  

Now in my final year as a senior, I'm diving deeper into understanding systems. I believe if I understand the details of how a system works I can design a better system. I'm doing this through my coursework, taking OS, Distributed Systems, and Networks, and through my senior thesis with Dr. Keyser in the Aggie Graphics Group, researching into different parallelization strategies and caching mechanisms to speed up performance for simulations with millions of particles.  

Currently I'm looking for a full-time job starting after May 2027 within distributed systems, systems optimization, and networks. If you are interested in my profile feel free to reach out.
